3What the app does

Close Cords reads the track you're playing in Apple Music and, when you turn broadcasting on, shares it with other Close Cords users within Bluetooth range. It also shows you what people near you are playing. Playback controls in the app operate your own Apple Music account.

The app needs an Apple Music subscription to be useful. We don't provide music; the app connects to an account you already have.

5Broadcasting to people nearby

Broadcasting is off until you turn it on. When it's on, anyone running Close Cords within Bluetooth range can see your alias or display name and the track you're playing. You understand and accept that:

  • Bluetooth range isn't precise. Typically it's around 10–15 metres (30–50 feet), but walls, crowds, and device differences change it, and we can't guarantee who is or isn't in range.
  • Anyone with the app in range can see what you share. It isn't limited to friends or contacts.
  • Bluetooth signals can in principle be picked up by other Bluetooth devices, not only Close Cords.

You can turn broadcasting off at any time from the radar button or the Profile tab. Details of what is shared are in the Privacy Policy.
